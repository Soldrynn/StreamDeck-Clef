Clef 0.1.0.1

Windows 11 x64 | Stream Deck 7.1+ | Stream Deck +

Playback Controls
- Press or touch: play or pause.
- Rotate: play the previous or next track.

Volume Controls
- Press or touch: mute or unmute Apple Music for Windows.
- Rotate: change only Apple Music for Windows volume by 1-10% per step.

Each placed control keeps its own settings and reconnects automatically.

Album art appears when Windows makes it available. After a dial skip, the
plugin can briefly pause and resume playback to refresh delayed artwork.
